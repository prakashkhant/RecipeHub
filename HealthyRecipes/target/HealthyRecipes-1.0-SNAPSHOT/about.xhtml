About Healthy Recipes

Welcome to Healthy Recipe Hub — your perfect place to discover delicious, easy-to-make and nutritious recipes. We believe eating healthy should never feel boring! 🍽✨

Our mission is to provide:

  • Healthy & Pure Vegetarian Recipes 🥗
  • Category-wise dishes like Breakfast, Dinner & more 🍛
  • Nutrition-focused and simple ingredients 🌱
  • Step-by-step cooking guidance 👨‍🍳

This mini project is developed by Khant Prakashkumar using Java EE, JSF, EJB, and MySQL. The purpose of this platform is to help people make smarter and healthier food choices every day.
